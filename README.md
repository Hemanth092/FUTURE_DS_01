# FUTURE_DS_01 — Business Sales Performance Analytics

## Project Overview
This project analyzes business sales data to identify revenue trends, top-selling products, high-performing categories, regions, and customer segments.

## Objectives
- Analyze overall sales performance
- Identify yearly and monthly sales trends
- Find top-selling products
- Analyze sales by category and region
- Identify high-performing sub-categories and customer segments
- Provide actionable business recommendations

## Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook
- Power BI

## Dataset
The dataset contains order, customer, product, category, region, and sales information.

## Key Insights
- Total Sales: **$2,261,536.78**
- Total Orders: **4,922**
- Total Customers: **793**
- Average Order Value: **$459.48**
- Best-performing Category: **Technology**
- Best-performing Region: **West**
- Best-performing Sub-Category: **Phones**
- Highest Sales Year: **2018**

## Recommendations
- Focus on high-performing Technology products.
- Strengthen sales strategies in the West region.
- Analyze seasonal demand and prepare inventory for high-sales months.
- Promote high-performing products and categories.
- Use customer and regional sales patterns to improve targeted marketing.
